The @ Bliss Path

Take back the helm.

You may already recognize that there is a voice inside you that is not always who you truly are.

Thoughts arise. Emotions arise. Patterns take over. Sometimes we watch ourselves react in ways we did not consciously choose.

If you are the captain of your ship, you may begin to see that much of the time, the ship is running on autopilot.

The @ Bliss Path is the process of learning how to take back the helm.

Before You Begin

"I recognize that there is a voice inside me, and that this voice is not my authentic self. I recognize that, at times, and perhaps much of the time, it influences or directs the way I live.

If I am the captain of my ship, I can see that the ship is often running on autopilot, following patterns shaped by conditioning, habits, fears, and past experiences.

And I sincerely want to take back the helm."

If you can sincerely say this, you are ready to begin.
Welcome to The @ Bliss Path.

Regulate

Awareness alone is not enough.

You may recognize a pattern intellectually and still find yourself repeating it.

That is because our patterns do not exist only in our thoughts. They can also be experienced through our emotions, nervous system, breath, posture, physical sensations, habits, and the Soma.

This is why Regulate is an essential part of The @ Bliss Methodology and an important part of beginning The @ Bliss Path.

We start learning to notice what happens within us before, during, and after a reaction.

What happens to my breathing?
What happens in my body?
What emotion appears?
What thought follows?
What do I do next?

As awareness grows, we begin creating space between the automatic reaction and the person observing it.

Somatic Breathwork is one of the practices we use to begin exploring that space.

Regulate → Release → Repattern → Integrate.

Regulation also extends beyond breathwork. As The @ Bliss Path develops, we explore how sleeping, eating, movement, attention, and other everyday practices influence the Soma and the way we experience life.

The goal is not to regulate yourself perfectly.
It is to become increasingly conscious of what is happening within you.

What Comes Next

The Path goes deeper.

The @ Bliss Path is not a breathwork program. Breathwork is one practice within a much broader journey of self-observation, regulation, contemplation, mindful living, coherence, resonance, philosophy, and remembering.

As you continue, you will begin exploring your thoughts, emotions, Soma, behaviors, inner voices, patterns, relationships, desires, habits, and the way you experience yourself and the world around you.

You will encounter practices.
You will encounter questions.
You will encounter ideas carried through different wisdom traditions.

And you will be encouraged not simply to believe them, but to explore them through your own experience.

The Methodology gives us the map. The Path is how we travel it.
