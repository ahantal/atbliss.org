The @ Bliss Path

The Path of Remembering

A Journey Back to Self

Maybe you came here because of stress.

Maybe everything in your life seems fine on the outside, yet something within you does not feel right.

Maybe you have begun to notice the same thoughts, the same fears, the same reactions, or the same patterns of behavior appearing again and again.

Or maybe you simply sense that there is something deeper within you.

This is where the @ Bliss Path begins.

Not by telling you what you should believe.

By turning your Attention inward.

Because before we try to change ourselves, we need to begin seeing what is actually happening within us.

We cannot change what we do not notice

Most of us experience ourselves as one single, unchanging “I.”

But when we look a little more closely, we may begin to notice very different inner states appearing throughout the day.

Sometimes we are calm and understanding.
Sometimes angry.
Sometimes afraid.
Sometimes we want to control.
Sometimes we doubt ourselves.

At one moment we may act from love, and only a few minutes later an entirely different voice may take control.

This perspective parallels an observation found in ancient traditions of inner inquiry and in G.I. Gurdjieff's Fourth Way teaching: although we experience ourselves as a single “I,” different inner “I”s may influence our thoughts and actions at different moments.

At @ Bliss, we approach this from a very simple place:

First, see.
Then, notice.
Then, create the space in which you can choose.

Attention

At the center of this Path is Attention.

Because a thought we do not notice can carry us away.

An emotion we do not recognize can determine our reaction.

A Soma we are not listening to may already be trying to tell us something.

But the moment we turn our Attention inward, another possibility appears.

We can notice a thought as it arises.
We can notice an emotion while we are feeling it.
We can notice a change within the Soma as it is happening.

And perhaps, for the first time, we can create a small space between automatic reaction and conscious choice.

Awakening begins in that space.

The Path

The @ Bliss Path unfolds through six interconnected stages.

This is not a checklist.

Nor is it a linear system in which we complete one stage and move permanently to the next.

It is a Path we may move through again and again, with the possibility of going a little deeper each time we return.

Awaken→Regulate→Become Coherent→Resonate→Enjoy→Remember

01

Awaken

If there is a voice inside you speaking to you, telling you what you should do, that voice cannot be the real you. It cannot be your Essence.

Awakening begins when you learn to observe your thoughts, patterns, reactions, and inner voices instead of automatically assuming they are you.

So the first practice is not about changing yourself.

It is about seeing yourself.

As you begin to notice the first signs appearing in your thoughts, emotions, and Soma, you may begin to recognize an automatic reaction before it takes control.

We call this first practice of observation and mapping Self-Mapping.

Knowing what you are not is the beginning of remembering your Essence.

Begin Self-Mapping →

02

Regulate

Noticing is the beginning.

But noticing alone is not always enough.

Sometimes the mind understands what is happening while the Soma continues to carry the old response.

This is why we learn to turn our Attention toward the Soma.

To the breath.
To posture.
To tension.
To bodily sensations.

When we notice the first signs of an unwanted reaction, instead of automatically following it, we can learn to pause, breathe, and create a small space between stimulus and response.

That space gives us the possibility of choosing.

Regulate → Release → Repattern → Integrate

Somatic Breathwork is one of the tools we use at this stage.

03

Become Coherent

Regulation creates space.

Coherence explores bringing the heart, mind, Soma, and emotions into greater alignment.

Through meditation, breathwork, focused Attention, and heart-centered practices, we learn to cultivate greater coherence within ourselves.

As the practice deepens, we may begin exploring different states of consciousness.

We explore how to direct our Attention more consciously, move beyond inner noise, and cultivate deeper alignment between heart, mind, and Soma.

As the relationship within us begins to change, we may also begin to experience changes in how we relate to ourselves, to others, and to life.

Becoming coherent with the Universe gradually stops being something we practice.

It begins to become a way of living.

04

Resonate

As we become more coherent, we become more aware of how we resonate.

Where am I directing my Attention?
What am I feeding?
Which thoughts am I repeating?
From what emotional state am I acting?
And what am I bringing into life?

Through meditation, visualization, conscious intention, and other contemplative practices, we explore the relationship between our inner state and the reality we experience.

We begin to explore creation not merely as a mental wish, but as a process in which our Attention, intention, emotions, and actions begin moving in the same direction.

Over time, the question shifts from:

“How can I make this happen?”

to:

“What do I need to become aligned with?”

Creation is not only about what we want. It is also about where we direct our Attention and the inner state from which we direct it.

05

Enjoy

The Path is not an escape from the human experience.

It is an invitation to experience it more fully.

Life offers us extraordinary beauty, tastes, sensations, relationships, creativity, laughter, love, and joy.

We do not have to give these things up in order to awaken.

The question is:

While I am enjoying life, am I losing my Attention?
Or can I remain both the one who is living and the one who is watching the experience unfold?

When we can remain both the one who is living and the one who is watching, without completely losing ourselves inside an experience, we do not have to give up what life offers us.

On the contrary, we may experience it more fully.

This Journey can also be joyful.

Be present.
Be with yourself.
Enjoy being human.

06

Remember

The @ Bliss Path is not about becoming someone new.

It is about remembering who you truly are.

As the voices, patterns, fears, identities, and conditioning that once seemed to define you begin to lose their hold, it may become easier to recognize what lies deeper.

You begin to remember your connection with others, with life, and ultimately with the One.

But Remembering is not a state we reach once and never lose again.

It is the act of returning to ourselves again and again within life.

As the light of our Attention touches thoughts, emotions, fears, and patterns that we once lived without seeing, what was invisible begins to become visible.

What was unnoticed becomes noticed.
What is noticed enters Consciousness.

When our Attention wanders, we may forget.

When we return our Attention to ourselves, to the present moment, and to what lies deeper, we can remember again.

Perhaps the Path does not end when you remember.

Perhaps it continues when you accompany someone else as they remember their own Essence.

Attention is the guardian of remembering.

So where do we begin?

Somewhere much simpler.

By noticing one “I” within you that makes life more difficult.

The Angry I.
The Anxious I.
The I That Wants to Control.
The Self-Doubting I.
The Critical I.
The Victim I.

It does not matter which one it is.

For now, choose just one.

Do not try to change it.
Do not judge it.
Do not suppress it.

Simply begin mapping it.

Begin Self-Mapping →

You are not a prisoner of what you think and feel. With every breath, every moment of awareness, and every choice, you have the capacity to reshape your inner state.
