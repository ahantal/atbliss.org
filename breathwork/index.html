The Experience

Soma+IQ™ The Journey

An independently developed practice, offered as one of the experiences within @ Bliss.

The Journey is a full inner voyage, designed to support emotional release, awaken insight, and reconnect you with your deeper purpose and intuitive intelligence. This is where breath becomes a bridge to the subconscious, the soul, and the parts of you that long to be seen and integrated.

Why We Begin With The Soma

Awareness does not live only in the mind.

We often experience a pattern before we fully understand it.
A thought appears.
An emotion follows.
The breath changes.
The chest tightens.
The shoulders rise.
The body prepares to react.

At @ Bliss, we use the word Soma to describe the lived body, the body as it is experienced from within.

Somatic Breathwork gives us a way to begin working with that experience directly. Through conscious breathing and embodied awareness, we begin learning to notice what is happening within us and to relate differently to what arises.

Regulate → Release → Repattern → Integrate.

Experience Before Explanation

You do not need to believe anything.

The @ Bliss Path asks us to observe ourselves deeply. But before asking you to study a philosophy or understand a concept, we invite you to experience something for yourself.

For this reason, Soma+IQ™ The Journey is one of the first experiences on The @ Bliss Path.

You do not need to understand everything that happens.
You do not need to arrive at a particular conclusion.
Experience. Observe. Stay curious.

Contraindications

Soma+IQ™ Breathwork is a personal growth practice and not a substitute for psychotherapy. It is not suitable for pregnant women or individuals with serious medical conditions such as heart issues, high blood pressure, glaucoma, epilepsy, recent surgery, or major psychiatric diagnoses. If unsure, please consult your doctor. Asthma patients should bring their inhaler and seek medical advice before participating.

By signing up for a session you agree to these terms

I acknowledge that Soma+IQ™ Breathwork is a deep and powerful process. It involves rapid and prolonged breathing that is designed to activate intense emotional responses. I have notified the practitioner of any physical injuries, mental or psychological conditions I have. I engage in this experience willingly and take full responsibility for my own physical, mental and emotional experiences during and after the session.

If you have questions or concerns specifically regarding Soma+IQ™ Breathwork, please contact support@drsamirmujagic.com.

Before Your Session

Think of a voice within you that sometimes takes the helm.

We all have moments when a particular voice, reaction, or version of ourselves seems to take over.

Perhaps it is the angry voice. The fearful one. The jealous one. The defensive one. The controlling one. The one that needs to be right. The one that shuts down. The one that says something you later wish you had not said.

Think of a recent moment when one of these voices took the helm and led you somewhere your authentic self would not have chosen to go.

Perhaps afterward you felt regret. Perhaps you hurt someone you did not want to hurt. Perhaps you blamed yourself for something that was not really yours to carry. Perhaps you simply wondered: "Why did I do that?"

Choose one specific moment. Don’t analyze it too much. We simply want you to remember what happened just before that voice took control.

01

Don’t worry about finding the perfect name for it. How would you describe the version of you that appeared in that moment?

  • The angry one
  • The fearful one
  • The jealous one
  • The defensive one
  • The controller
  • The critic
  • The one who needed approval
  • The one who needed to be right
  • The one who shut down
  • Something else

Give that voice a name that makes sense to you.

02

Think about the moment immediately before your reaction. What happened? What was said? What did you see, hear, remember, or experience that seemed to activate this voice?

Describe the moment as specifically as you can.

03

Before the voice fully took the helm, there may have been a thought, even a very quick one. What went through your mind?

  • “They don’t respect me.”
  • “I’m going to lose this.”
  • “This isn’t fair.”
  • “I need to defend myself.”
  • “I’m not good enough.”
  • “They are going to hurt me.”

What was your thought, in your own words?

04

Before the reaction fully took over, what emotion appeared?

  • Fear
  • Anger
  • Shame
  • Jealousy
  • Sadness
  • Frustration
  • Rejection
  • Helplessness
  • Anxiety
  • Something else

If there was more than one emotion, list whatever you remember.

05

Before or as the voice took control, what changed in your body? Did your breathing change? Did your chest tighten? Did you feel heat? Did your stomach contract? Did your shoulders rise? Did your heart begin beating faster? Did you become restless, heavy, numb, frozen, or tense?

Try to remember the earliest physical signal you can.

06

What happened once that voice had the helm? What did you say? What did you do? What did you avoid doing? What decision did you make? How did you treat yourself or someone else?
