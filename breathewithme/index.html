My Story

My journey actually started with philosophy.

Most of you receiving this page know me personally.

And many of you know that I have been on a journey of questioning, exploring, and trying to understand ourselves and this strange and beautiful experience we call life for a very long time.

As most of you know, I started my undergraduate studies at Boğaziçi University as a Philosophy major before transferring to Business Administration.

You may also have heard me say many times that beginning my university journey as a philosophy student was one of the finest things that ever happened to me.

Something happened to my young mind during those years.

A door opened that has never closed since.

It was the door to questioning what I thought I knew. Questioning the things I had accepted as fact. Questioning my assumptions, beliefs, perceptions, and eventually questioning new knowledge as I encountered it.

That curiosity has stayed with me throughout my life.

I have meditated for more than thirty years and explored different practices, philosophies, traditions, and ways of understanding ourselves and our relationship with life.

But more recently, I discovered something that affected me deeply:

Somatic Breathwork.

I had experienced profound states through meditation and other deeper ceremonial practices before. What surprised me about Somatic Breathwork was discovering how much could become accessible through something so fundamental and already within us:

Our own breath.

No substance.
Just breathing.

The experience made me curious enough that I wanted to understand what was happening, learn the modality properly, and experience it more deeply.

That curiosity eventually led me to train and become a Soma+IQ™ Somatic Breathwork Practitioner.

And now I want to share the experience with you.

Why I’m Inviting You

Why I’m Inviting You

I’m not asking you to believe anything.
I’m not trying to convince you of anything.

I simply want you to experience it.

One of the things that fascinated me about this practice was experiencing how consciously working with our own breath can influence how we feel and how we experience our nervous system.

And that brought me back to something I’ve increasingly come to appreciate:

The mind is not the only place we carry our lives.

We deal with pressure every day.

Work. Family. Relationships. Responsibilities. Money. Expectations. Uncertainty. Things that happened yesterday, and things we’re already worrying about tomorrow.

These experiences affect the way we think and feel.

But they also affect the Soma, the body.

Stress can show up in our nervous system, our breathing, muscular tension, posture, and the physical sensations we carry with us.

Sometimes we move through that pressure naturally.
Sometimes we don’t.

We hold it in. We tighten. We suppress what we feel. We continue functioning while the pressure continues to build.

And this is where Somatic Breathwork became particularly interesting to me.

Somatic Breathwork

Sometimes We Can Let the Soma Speak

Somatic Breathwork uses conscious breathing together with movement, sound, and emotional expression to bring our attention away from constantly analyzing what is happening and toward actually experiencing what is happening within the body.

We spend an extraordinary amount of our lives inside our heads.

Thinking.
Analyzing.
Explaining.
Trying to understand why we feel something.

During the practice, we can temporarily approach things differently.

We don’t always have to ask:

Why am I feeling this?
Where did this come from?
What does this mean?

Sometimes we can simply notice it.

Feel it.
Breathe with it.
Allow it.
Express it.
And let it move.

Feel it. Allow it. Express it. Let it move.

This is part of a process I think of as:

Regulate → Release → Repattern → Integrate.

And perhaps one of the most interesting things about it is how simple the fundamental tool is.

Your own breath.

The Experience

Come Experience It With Me

This is why I’m sending you this page.

I would love for some of my friends to experience the modality that has become an important part of my own journey.

I’ll guide you through the full Soma+IQ™ The Journey, an approximately 90-minute Somatic Breathwork experience using conscious connected breathing, music, movement, sound, and emotional expression.

There is nothing you need to achieve.

And there is absolutely nothing you need to believe.

Your experience may be very different from someone else’s, and that’s perfectly fine.

My only invitation is:

Experience. Observe. Stay curious.
