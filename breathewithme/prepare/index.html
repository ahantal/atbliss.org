Come Breathe With Me

How to prepare.

You don’t need to know how to do this, and you don’t need any previous experience. A little preparation just helps you settle in and get the most out of our time together.

  • Monday, September 21, 2026
  • 9:30 PM Turkish Time (2:30 PM ET)

What You’ll Need

  • Comfortable Clothing

    Loose, comfortable clothing you can breathe and move in. Nothing restrictive.

  • A Place to Lie Down

    A mat, a rug, or your bed. Give yourself enough space to stretch out and move freely.

  • Headphones

    Music is a big part of the experience. Headphones help you stay inward and undistracted.

  • A Quiet, Private Space

    Somewhere you won’t be interrupted for the full 90 minutes.

  • Before You Begin

    Try not to eat a large meal 1 hour before the session.

    Turn off phone notifications and anything else that might interrupt you, and join Zoom a few minutes early so you have time to settle in before we begin together.

What to Expect

Approximately 15 minutes

Arrive

We’ll arrive together, get comfortable, go over what to expect, prepare the body and breath, and create some space to settle into the experience before we begin.

Approximately 60 minutes

Breathe

I’ll guide you through the full Soma+IQ™ The Journey, using conscious connected breathing together with music, movement, sound, and emotional expression.

This is the active part of the experience.

The breath gradually builds activation, and rather than immediately trying to calm or control what arises, we create space to allow the experience.

Movement, sound, emotion, physical sensation, or other forms of expression may arise.

Nothing needs to be forced.

There is no particular experience you are supposed to have.

Your experience is your own.

Approximately 15 minutes

Share & Integrate

We’ll slow things down, come back together, and leave space to share.

There is no obligation to speak.

You can share what you experienced, ask a question, listen to others, or simply stay present and allow the experience to settle.

This final part gives us some space before we return to the rest of our day or, for those joining later in the evening, head toward sleep.

Come as you are.

You don’t need to know what you’re supposed to do or what you’re supposed to feel. I’ll take care of the rest.
