Come Breathe With Me

How to prepare.

You don’t need to know how to do this, and you don’t need any previous experience. A little preparation just helps you settle in and get the most out of our time together.

Wear something comfortable

Loose, comfortable clothing you can breathe and move in. Nothing restrictive.

Find a quiet, private space

Somewhere you won’t be interrupted for the full 90 minutes. Let the people you live with know you’ll be unavailable.

Set up a comfortable place to lie down

A mat, a rug, or your bed. You’ll be lying down for most of the session, so give yourself enough space to stretch out and move freely.

Use headphones if you can

Music is a big part of the experience. Headphones help you stay inward and undistracted, especially if you’re not entirely alone in the space.

Have water nearby

Keep a glass or bottle of water within reach for after the active breathing.

Avoid a heavy meal beforehand

Try not to eat a large meal in the two to three hours before we begin. Something light is fine if you need it.

Turn off what might interrupt you

Phone notifications, doorbells, other browser tabs. Give yourself permission to fully step away for this hour and a half.

Join Zoom a few minutes early

It gives you time to settle in, get comfortable, and arrive before we begin together as a group.

Come as you are.

You don’t need to know what you’re supposed to do or what you’re supposed to feel. I’ll take care of the rest.
