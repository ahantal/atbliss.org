How to prepare.

You don’t need to know how to do this, and you don’t need any previous experience. A little preparation just helps you settle in and get the most out of our time together.

  • Monday, September 28, 2026
  • 9:30 PM Turkish Time (2:30 PM ET)

What You’ll Need

  • Comfortable Clothing

    Loose, comfortable clothing you can breathe and move in. Nothing restrictive.

  • A Place to Lie Down

    A mat, a rug, or your bed. Give yourself enough space to stretch out and move freely.

  • Headphones

    Music is a big part of the experience. Headphones help you stay inward and undistracted.

  • A Quiet, Private Space

    Somewhere you won’t be interrupted for the full 90 minutes.

  • Before You Begin

    Try not to eat a large meal 1 hour before the session.

    Turn off phone notifications and anything else that might interrupt you, and join Zoom a few minutes early so you have time to settle in before we begin together.

What to Expect

  1. Arrive

    We’ll settle in together and I’ll walk you through what the experience ahead will feel like.

  2. Breathe

    You’ll lie down comfortably and be guided through the Soma+IQ™ breathwork itself.

  3. Rest & Integrate

    We’ll slow things down, come back together, and leave space to rest and share.

Come as you are.

You don’t need to know what you’re supposed to do or what you’re supposed to feel. I’ll take care of the rest.
