Prepare for Your Session

A few simple things to know before we breathe together.

Find a comfortable space. Bring your headphones. And come as you are.

What you’ll need

  • Comfortable Clothing

    Loose, comfortable clothing you can breathe and move in. Nothing restrictive.

  • A Comfortable Place to Lie Down

    A mat, yoga mat, rug, or another comfortable surface where you can lie on your back and stretch out freely.

  • Headphones or Earbuds

    Music is a big part of the experience. Headphones or earbuds help you stay inward and undistracted, and hear the guidance clearly.

  • A Quiet, Private Space

    Somewhere you can breathe comfortably without being interrupted for the full 90 minutes.

  • Zoom & Your Camera

    Make sure you have access to Zoom and that your camera and audio are working before we begin. During the breathwork you’ll be lying on your back, so position your camera so your upper body, including your chest and belly, can be seen comfortably. This allows the facilitator to observe your breathing and somatic response and guide the session live.

    Please turn off Zoom background blur, virtual backgrounds, and other camera background effects before we begin.

Give your body some space

Try not to eat a large meal 1 hour before the session.

A little space in the body helps the breath move freely and keeps you comfortable throughout the practice.

What to expect

  1. Arrive

    Approximately 15 minutes

    We’ll arrive together, get comfortable, go over what to expect, prepare the body and breath, and settle into the experience before we begin.

  2. Breathe

    Approximately 60 minutes

    You’ll lie comfortably on your back while being guided through Soma+IQ™ The Journey, using conscious connected breathing together with music, movement, sound, and emotional expression. The breath gradually builds activation, and rather than trying to calm or control what arises, we create space to allow it. Nothing needs to be forced. Your experience is your own.

  3. Share & Integrate

    Approximately 15 minutes

    We’ll slow things down, come back together, and leave space to share. There is no obligation to speak. You can share, ask a question, listen, or simply stay present and let the experience settle. This final part gives us some space before we return to the rest of our day or, for those joining later in the evening, head toward sleep.

A few minutes before

  • Find your quiet space
  • Have your mat or comfortable surface ready
  • Put your headphones or earbuds nearby
  • Turn off phone notifications and anything else that might interrupt you
  • Open Zoom and check your camera and audio
  • Turn off background blur and virtual backgrounds
  • Position your device so your upper body can be seen while lying down
  • Join a few minutes early and give yourself time to arrive without rushing
