About Our Methodology The @ Bliss Path Self-Mapping Manifesto

Can you feel it?

You may not have the words for it yet. Only a feeling. A pull toward something you cannot name, a sense that beneath all the chaos and the noise and the pain, something beautiful is waiting.

You may feel it deeply and still not know why, what it means, or what to do with it.

And at the same time, you may be living with stress, confusion, or a sense of feeling lost or rootless.

If any of this feels familiar, you are exactly where you need to be.

Welcome!

What matters is how you resonate

Everything begins in the body and in what you think. The state of your nervous system and the quality of your thoughts shape how you feel. And how you feel shapes how you resonate. Your resonance is not simply something that happens to you. It is influenced by everything you carry, everything you practice, and everything you choose. This is where the journey begins.

Our Mission

To help humanity remember.

@ Bliss exists to help people awaken to who they truly are, learn to consciously shape how they resonate, and remember their connection to one another and to the One.

Through awareness, practice, shared experience, and the wisdom carried across traditions, we seek to help each other live with greater coherence, compassion, joy, and universal unconditional love.

"We are not here to tell you what to believe. We are here to help each other remember what we already know by heart."

Our Vision

A world that remembers.

A world where we understand that how we resonate shapes how we live, and choose consciously to resonate with Light. Where compassion and non-harm are not simply teachings, but lived realities. Not as law. Not as religion. But as the natural expression of a humanity that has remembered its Oneness.

A world where we know this not simply as an idea, but in our bodies, our thoughts, and the texture of our daily lives. Where synchronicity is recognized as the fingerprint of a deeper order. Where serendipity reminds us that life may be responding in ways we do not always understand. Where we learn not to force our way through life, but to become more coherent with it.

Where we no longer say, "May the Force be with you."

We say, "May we be with the Force."

Manifesto

What We Know by Heart

If there is a voice inside you speaking to you, telling you things, that voice cannot be the real you. It cannot be your true Self.

Thoughts arise. Fears arise. Judgments, memories, desires, and reactions arise. We experience them, yet we can also observe them.

This simple realization opens a space between the voice that speaks and the one who listens.

As we begin to notice our thoughts, emotions, and changes within our Soma, we also begin to recognize our automatic reactions earlier.

The moment we notice, a space opens between reaction and choice.

This is where awakening begins.

But awakening is not enough.

To remember our awakening, we need a Path. To live what the Path asks of us, we need practice. And to observe the ceaseless mechanism of associations within us, we need Attention.

Working through our fears, desires, habits, and conditioning, this mechanism can make us believe once again that we are that voice and cause us to forget, again and again, the Truth we know by heart.

Attention is the guardian of remembering.

Universal unconditional love is not an idea learned from outside.

It arises naturally from within when we remember our Oneness.

Each of us is a different expression of the One. Though we may appear to live separate lives, we arise from the same Source.

When we resonate in Light, we begin to live not only for ourselves, but for the well-being of the whole.

An ancient principle appears in different forms across cultures and wisdom traditions:

Do not do to others what you would not want done to you.
Do not harm those who do not harm you.
Do not destroy those who do not threaten your life.

This is not a rule imposed from outside.

It is a way of living that arises naturally when we know by heart that we are One.

This ancient Truth has been expressed through different doors across cultures, religions, philosophies, and wisdom traditions.

Sufism, Shamanism, Buddhism, Taoism, Kabbalah, Christian mysticism, and the Vedic traditions may speak different languages, yet they point toward the same deep search.

@ Bliss does not seek to replace any of them.

Its purpose is to make visible again the consciousness of Oneness and the universal compassion we can find at the heart of them all.

@ Bliss is a space created for remembering together.

The question we ask here is:

"What is it that I do not know or have forgotten that could bring me, and all of us, a little closer to the One, to Love, and to Truth?"

Tune your frequency.
Invite the light into your life.

How It Works

The @ Bliss Methodology

The @ Bliss Path unfolds through six interconnected stages. This is not a checklist, but an ongoing journey. Each stage builds upon the one before it.

Awaken→ Regulate→ Become Coherent→ Resonate→ Enjoy→ Remember
01
Awaken

If there is a voice inside you telling you what to do, that voice cannot be you.

Awakening begins when you learn to observe your thoughts, patterns, reactions, and inner voices instead of automatically identifying with them.

As you begin to notice the first signs appearing in your thoughts, emotions, and Soma, you begin to recognize an automatic reaction before it takes control.

Knowing what you are not is the beginning of remembering your Essence.

02
Regulate

Awareness alone is not enough. The patterns we recognize in the mind also live in the body, the Soma.

Awareness shows us what is happening. Attention allows us to stay with it.

To begin regulating, we learn to direct our Attention toward the Soma. We notice the early signals that appear in the breath, posture, muscular tension, and physical sensations.

When we notice these early signals, we learn to pause instead of reacting automatically, to breathe, and to create a small space between stimulus and response.

Through Somatic Breathwork and other embodied practices, including mindful eating, mindful sleeping, and conscious movement, we learn to work with the nervous system.

We create the conditions in which old patterns can begin to release and new patterns can emerge.

We begin to experience that regulating our nervous system can change how we think, feel, and respond.

Attention is where Awakening begins to settle into the Soma.

Regulate → Release → Repattern → Integrate.

Explore Somatic Breathwork →
03
Become Coherent

Regulation creates space. Coherence brings us into alignment.

Through meditation, breath, focused attention, and heart-centered practices, we learn to bring the heart, mind, body, and emotions into greater coherence.

As the practice deepens, we begin to explore different states of consciousness through breath, meditation, heart-centered practices, and practices intended to support pineal activation.

We explore how to direct our Attention more consciously, move beyond inner noise, and cultivate deeper alignment between heart, mind, and body.

As greater coherence begins to emerge within us, we may also experience changes in how we relate to ourselves, to others, and to life.

Over time, coherence becomes more than something we practice.

It becomes a way we live.

04
Resonate

As we become more coherent, we become more aware of how we resonate.

Through meditation, visualization, conscious intention, and other contemplative practices, we explore the relationship between our inner state and the reality we experience.

From the deeper inner states cultivated through coherence, we begin to understand more deeply the importance of where we direct our Attention. What we give our Attention to is also what we feed.

We begin to explore creation not merely as a mental wish or thought, but as a process in which our Attention, intention, emotions, and actions begin moving in the same direction.

We begin to recognize that how we resonate is shaped not only by meditation or intention, but also by how we eat, sleep, move, consume, relate, think, and direct our Attention.

The question gradually shifts from:

“How can I make this happen?”

to:

“What do I need to become coherent with?”

We begin to consciously shape how we resonate, not by trying to control life, but by becoming more aware of what we are bringing into it.

Creation is not only about what we want. It is also about where we direct our Attention and the inner state from which we direct it.

05
Enjoy

The Path is not an escape from the human experience. It is an invitation to experience it more fully.

Life offers us extraordinary beauty, tastes, sensations, relationships, creativity, laughter, love, and joy. We do not need to reject these things in order to awaken.

What is needed here is to recognize whether we are being consumed by our desires.

While I am enjoying life, am I losing my Attention, or can I remain both the one who is living and the one who is watching the experience unfold?

When we can remain both the one who is living and the one who is watching, without completely losing ourselves inside an experience, we do not have to give up what life offers us. On the contrary, we may experience it more fully.

This Journey can be enjoyable too.

When we live an experience not by forgetting ourselves, but by remembering our Self, we become free.

Responsibility, discipline, pain, and challenge still exist. But the Path itself does not need to become another struggle.

Be present. Remain within yourself. Enjoy being human.

06
Remember

The @ Bliss Path is not about becoming someone new.

It is about remembering who you truly are.

As the voices, patterns, fears, identities, and conditioning that once seemed to define you begin to lose their hold, it becomes easier to recognize what lies deeper.

You begin to remember your connection to others, to life, and ultimately to the One.

Remembering is not a state we reach once and never lose again. It is the act of returning to ourselves again and again within life.

As the light of our Attention touches thoughts, emotions, fears, and patterns that we once lived without seeing, what was invisible begins to become visible.

What was unnoticed becomes noticed. What is noticed enters Consciousness.

When our Attention wanders, we may forget. When we return our Attention to ourselves, to the present moment, and to what lies deeper, we can remember again.

Perhaps the Path does not end when you remember.

Perhaps it continues when you accompany another as they remember their own Self.

Attention is the guardian of remembering.

The Realizations

Levels of Awareness

These are not ranks, achievements, or labels. Along the Path, there are things we first understand with the mind. Then we begin to notice them within our own experience. What is noticed enters Consciousness. Over time, something we once knew intellectually can become something we know by heart and begin to live as a realization.

01
Truth

There is a voice inside you speaking to you, and that voice is not the real you.

This voice is shaped by your experiences, fears, habits, conditioning, and emotional patterns.

If you can hear the voice inside you, who is the one hearing it?

You are still the captain of the ship. But the ship may often be moving on autopilot. Until we notice it, we may believe that we are the ones choosing the direction.

Realizing this is Truth.

And there is another Truth waiting to be discovered:

You can take the helm again.

This is why the @ Bliss Path exists.

02
Ability

Ability is knowing by heart that you have the capacity to shape your life.

You cannot control everything that happens in your life. But you have more Ability than you may realize in where you direct your Attention, how you respond, the intention with which you act, and the choices you make.

You can change patterns, make conscious and wise choices, set intentions with inner knowing, and use your own agency to transform how you resonate.

Bringing something into your life is no longer simply about wishing for it.

Your Attention, Awareness, intention, emotions, beliefs, and actions begin to flow together with the Universe.

You move from merely reacting to life toward consciously participating in it.

03
Oneness

Oneness is reached when you know by heart that beneath our apparent differences, we all come from the same Source and are deeply interconnected.

Oneness is not only knowing that we are all connected. It is reaching the Consciousness through which you can recognize in another the same life you recognize within yourself.

From this understanding comes the Golden Rule:

Do not do to another what you would not have done to you.
Do not harm what does not harm you.
Do not destroy what does not threaten your life.

At this level, compassion is no longer simply something we are taught. It becomes the natural expression of knowing our oneness.

04
Unity

Unity is reached when you know by heart that the consciousness within you is not separate from the consciousness of the Creator, or from the consciousness within anyone else.

Those who lived before us, those who are alive today, and those who will be born in the future are expressions of the same greater consciousness.

There is the individual experience of being you.
But the Consciousness experiencing it is not limited to you alone.

To know this by heart is Unity.

Begin The Practice

The @ Bliss Path

The @ Bliss Path is free and open to everyone who genuinely wishes to learn it. But like every meaningful path, it asks something of you: time for yourself, practice, the completion of exercises along the way, and above all, a willingness to look inward with curiosity and honesty. There is no belief system you are required to adopt, but there is one idea you need to be ready to explore before beginning.

Before You Begin

Read the following statement carefully. Do not agree with it simply because you think you are supposed to. Take a moment and ask yourself whether you genuinely understand what it means, and whether you are ready to explore it through your own experience.

“I know that there is a voice inside me that tells me things, and that this voice is not my Essence.

I see that, at times, and perhaps much of the time, this voice works like an autopilot that influences or directs the way I live my life.

I can see that my past experiences, habits, and conditioning shape this voice, and with it, the direction the autopilot follows.

And I sincerely want to take the helm again.”

If you can say these words and are ready to explore them, we can begin.
Welcome to @ Bliss.

Founder

Ali C. Hantal
Founder, @ Bliss Foundation

Serial entrepreneur, technology expert, seeker of Truth, former national skier, Certified Somatic Breathwork Practitioner, and certified Lifeguard. Ali's journey has unfolded at the intersection of technological innovation and inner transformation.

Born in Istanbul, Ali's journey of questioning began in the Philosophy Department at Boğaziçi University. He later transferred to Business Administration, completed his MBA at the University of Baltimore, and entered the world of entrepreneurship.

Throughout his professional life, he has worked on technologies that bridge the physical and digital worlds. He founded XR Masters and has contributed to the development of open and shared technological foundations through international organizations including the Open AR Cloud Association, Open Geospatial Consortium, and Metaverse Standards Forum.

But his curiosity about understanding and transforming the outer world has always evolved alongside another exploration: the inner world.

Since his university years, he has explored different philosophical, ancient, and mystical traditions. He has practiced meditation for more than thirty years. Over time, he began to see that despite using different languages and symbols, many traditions point toward the same fundamental questions:

Who am I?
What connects us to one another?
And how can we know what we think we know, not only intellectually, but by heart and through lived experience?

Encountering Somatic Breathwork became an important turning point in this journey.

He began to discover that many things he had understood intellectually and known by heart could also have a counterpart within the Soma. Breath opened a new bridge between intellectual understanding and embodied experience.

@ Bliss Foundation emerged where technology, philosophy, more than thirty years of inner exploration, and this new exploration of the Soma began to meet.

Today, Ali facilitates individual and group Somatic Breathwork sessions and holds space for people exploring their own path back to Self.

But @ Bliss is not only about breathwork.

It is concerned with a larger question:

What is it that we do not know or have forgotten that could bring us a little closer to the One, to Love, and to Truth?
